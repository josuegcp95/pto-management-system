# Global Campus Solutions — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective:** January 1, 2026

## Quick Reference

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Annual allowance | 120 hours (15 days), granted January 1 |
| Workday | 8 hours, Monday through Friday |
| Minimum request | 1 full day (no partial days) |
| Advance notice | At least 7 calendar days |
| Long requests | More than 10 consecutive workdays requires extra manager review |
| Carryover | Up to 40 unused hours into the next year |
| Final decision | Your manager |

## Section 1: Eligibility

All employees of Global Campus Solutions receive PTO under this policy. PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or any other reason not covered by a separate leave type (see Section 9).

## Section 2: Annual Allowance

- Each employee receives **120 hours (15 workdays)** of PTO per calendar year.
- The full allowance is granted on **January 1**. PTO is not earned per pay period.
- Your balance cannot go below zero. You cannot request more hours than you have available.

## Section 3: How PTO Is Calculated

- A workday is **8 hours**. Only **Monday through Friday** count toward PTO.
- Weekends and company holidays (Section 4) inside a request are **not deducted** from your balance.
- PTO must be taken in **full days**. Partial-day requests are not available.
- **Example:** A request from Monday to Friday of the same week uses 40 hours. If that week includes a company holiday, it uses 32 hours.

## Section 5: Requesting PTO

1. Submit a request in the PTO Management System with a start date, end date, and an optional reason.
2. The request starts with a status of **Pending**. The hours are reserved as **pending hours** so they cannot be requested twice.
3. Your manager reviews the request and **approves** or **denies** it.
4. If approved, the hours move from pending to **used**. If denied, the hours return to your available balance.

**Request requirements**
- The start date must be on or before the end date.
- The start date cannot be in the past.
- The request must include at least one workday.
- A request cannot overlap with another pending or approved request of yours.

## Section 6: Advance Notice

- Submit requests at least **7 calendar days** before the start date.
- Requests with less notice can still be submitted, but they are **flagged** for your manager, who may deny them based on staffing needs.

## Section 7: Long Requests

- Requests of **more than 10 consecutive workdays** are **flagged** for additional manager review.
- Consider submitting long requests well in advance.

## Section 8: Carryover and Expiration

- Up to **40 unused hours** carry over into the next calendar year.
- Any unused hours above 40 **expire** on December 31.
- PTO is not paid out in place of time off.

## Section 9: Leave Not Covered by This Policy

The following are handled separately by Human Resources and are **not** part of PTO:

- Sick leave
- Parental leave (maternity, paternity, adoption)
- Bereavement leave
- Jury duty
- Military leave
- Medical or disability leave

For questions about these, contact Human Resources at **hr@globalcampussolutions.example**.

## Section 10: Approval and Changes

- Your **manager makes the final decision** on every PTO request.
- Flags from the system (such as short notice or long requests) are information for your manager, not automatic decisions.
- To change or cancel a request, contact your manager.

## Section 11: Questions

For questions not answered by this policy, contact your manager or Human Resources at **hr@globalcampussolutions.example**.
